## Sprint Retrospective

### What Went Well
- Decided to set Sprint duration to 4 weeks
- Allocated one week for each language, with first dedicated to creating cards, test cases and manual test execution.
- Worked with branches and pull requests.
- Linked branches with cards and closed cards directly from PRs.
- Better organized the project dashboard.

### What Didn't Go Well
- Task estimates were too optimistic.
- Didn’t took in to account the time required to verify and fix issues when the results were negative


### Improvement Actions
- Improve task estimation by considering verification and potential rework time.
- Reserve additional time for debugging and fixing issues.
- Review Sprint progress regularly to identify estimation risks earlier
